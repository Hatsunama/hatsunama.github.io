Research approach

Artificial Intelligence Research by Hatsunama

Hatsunama's artificial intelligence research examines AI models and the larger systems required to turn models into agents capable of useful work.

A model may produce a correct answer but still fail a real task. The surrounding system must provide context, choose a tool, construct arguments, execute the action, observe the result, recover from errors, and verify the outcome.

Model → inference → harness → tools → result

These layers can change task performance even when the underlying model stays the same. Evaluating an agent therefore requires looking at the full path from a request to a verified result, not only the model's final sentence.
