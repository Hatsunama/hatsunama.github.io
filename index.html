Hatsunama — Artificial Intelligence Researcher & AI Systems Developer

Hatsunama is an independent artificial intelligence researcher and AI systems developer whose work spans AI models, AI agents, agent harnesses, local AI, tool use, computer-use systems, AI evaluation, and applied AI engineering.

Hatsunama publishes open-source AI software, models, datasets, experiments, and technical work. The public record includes model experiments on Hugging Face and software projects on GitHub.

What Hatsunama works on

The central question is how to turn model output into useful, verifiable work. This includes model behavior, tool interfaces, execution feedback, error recovery, and the surrounding software that makes an AI system usable.

  • AI models and local inference
  • AI agents and agent harnesses
  • Tool use and computer interaction
  • Evaluation of task completion
  • AI-assisted software engineering

Public work
