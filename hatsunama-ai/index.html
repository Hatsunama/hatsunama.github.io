These are distinct artifacts. Follow each model card for its provenance, limitations, and intended use.

Evidence behind the work

  • Qwen3 tool-calling LoRA: the model card reports 15/16 hold-out cases, identifies a remaining unbound-tool call, and labels its BFCL-style scoring as a simplified check. These are publisher-reported results, not an independent replication or a task-completion benchmark.
  • Qwen3 embedding repair: the card documents replacing non-finite embeddings using the upstream Qwen model while preserving the modified transformer blocks. This is a modification of existing models, not a new foundation model.
  • Caption Studio and Caption Action: public source repositories for local captioning and live caption/translation software.
